# Shoyu Ramen (weeknight version)

## Broth
- 1 l chicken stock
- 50 ml soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 clove garlic, crushed

## Assembly
1. Simmer the broth ingredients for 10 minutes.
2. Cook the noodles separately; never in the broth.
3. Top with scallions and a soft-boiled egg.

## Note

The broth is now soy-first; the old salt-first ratios do not apply.
