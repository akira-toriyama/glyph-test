# Haiku

A tiny collection that grows one PR at a time.

## spring

    an old silent pond
    a frog jumps into the pond—
    water's sound

## summer

    in the cicada's cry
    no sign can foretell
    how soon it must die

## autumn

    on a withered branch
    a crow has settled down—
    autumn evening
